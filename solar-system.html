Process

How Was This Made?

  • Modeling & Animation (“Blender”): Each celestial body was modeled with accurate scale and orbital relationships.
  • Python Scripting: Automated orbits/rotations and rendered video/image outputs using code.
  • Materials & Atmosphere: Used realistic PBR textures and volumetric atmospheres.
  • Compositing: Combined shots in Blender’s Video Sequence Editor, color-graded for clarity.

Celestial Bodies

Explore each planet, the Sun, and the Moon—including key facts and rendered visuals.

The Sun

Our star: 4.6 billion years old, source of solar energy for the system.

Mercury

Closest to the Sun; smallest planet, extreme temperature swings.

Venus

Earth's twin in size, but shrouded in a thick, toxic atmosphere; the hottest planet.

Earth

The only known world to support life, with water and atmosphere.

Mars

The Red Planet, with a thin atmosphere and evidence of past water activity.

Jupiter

The largest planet, a gas giant with a prominent Great Red Spot.

Saturn

Known for its prominent ring system, composed of ice and rock particles.

Uranus

An ice giant with a unique sideways rotation and a faint ring system.

Neptune

The windiest planet, with speeds up to 2,100 km/h.

Pluto

A dwarf planet in the Kuiper belt, with a highly elliptical orbit.

Moon

Earth's only natural satellite, influencing tides and providing light at night.
